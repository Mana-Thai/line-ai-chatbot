# Mother's Day Match Cut Film v7

## Creative position

The film is a restrained 30-second circle: a mother gives what little she has,
the daughter learns the weight of earning, and the daughter returns care without
turning the moment into a spectacle. Faces are withheld so a customer can project
their own family into the story and so later photo personalization does not clash
with generated performers.

## Match-cut grammar

- Scene 1 and scene 3 share the same 9:16 crop, lens height, upper-left giving
  hand, lower-right receiving palm, center-lower-third contact point, wooden
  window, diagonal light, teal cloth and depth of field.
- Only three semantic variables change: child palm → elderly palm, mother's hand
  → adult daughter's hand, coin → plain white envelope.
- Camera drift is intentionally mirrored. Scene 1 moves inward by 4.2%; scene 3
  begins at the same visual scale and eases inward by 4.2%.
- No scene labels, exposition, subtitles, logos or visible tears are used.

## Image and type

- 35mm-inspired 24 fps cadence, restrained grain, halation, amber highlights and
  faded teal shadows.
- Final overlay only:
  `ตอนนี้…ถึงตาลูกดูแลแม่บ้างนะ`
- The text is rendered during editing with a Thai-capable typeface, not generated
  inside the image.

## Music direction

Original music only: intimate felt piano with a lullaby-like three-note motif,
airy Thai-flute-like breath texture and very restrained warm strings. It must not
quote or imitate an existing lullaby. The strongest lift is the envelope-to-palm
contact, followed by a warm unresolved-to-resolved afterglow under jasmine.
